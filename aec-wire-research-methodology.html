AEC Research Methodology

September 4, 2026 · By Izaiah Cottle

AEC research emphasizes traceable evidence, visible assumptions, reproducible methodology, risk analysis and clear separation between historical or simulated research and audited or live performance.

Research framework

AEC approaches this subject through documented assumptions, scenario analysis, risk awareness and evidence-based review. Conclusions should remain proportional to the quality and limits of the underlying evidence.
