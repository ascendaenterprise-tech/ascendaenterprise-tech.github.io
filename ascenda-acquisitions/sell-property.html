FAQ

Questions sellers ask

Why would I sell for less than a listing price?

A listing usually means repairs, showings, agent commissions, closing costs and months of carrying costs. An as-is sale trades some price for speed, certainty and no work. Compare both before you decide.

Who handles the closing?

A licensed Georgia attorney or title company handles closing, title and funds. You can use your own attorney too.

Might you assign the contract to another buyer?

Sometimes. If we plan to, the agreement will say so in plain language and you will initial that section. You always see this before you sign.

Do you buy houses with liens or back taxes?

Often yes. Payoffs are handled from the proceeds at closing, subject to title review.

Is there any obligation to ask for an offer?

None. Asking for an offer is free and does not obligate you.
