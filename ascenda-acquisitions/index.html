Selling your business?

We buy profitable, owner-operated businesses for our own account and keep them running. Your employees, customers and name stay in good hands.

What we look for

  • Steady earnings, generally $500K or more a year
  • Owners ready to retire or step back
  • Repeat customers with no single customer dominating
  • Simple operations: services, distribution, light manufacturing, healthcare support, property services, logistics
  • Metro Atlanta and the Hudson Valley, including Rockland and Orange County

How we work with sellers

  • A direct conversation, with confidentiality under an NDA before you share financials
  • A written letter of intent with price, structure and timing
  • Flexible structures, including seller notes and a paid transition period
  • Confirmatory due diligence handled by us, with your attorney and accountant welcome at every step
