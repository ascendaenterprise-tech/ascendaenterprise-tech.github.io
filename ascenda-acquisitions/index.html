Two ways we buy

Real estate and businesses

One buyer, two tracks, the same principles: speak plainly, put terms in writing, and close what we say we will close.

Properties

Houses, small multifamily and land. Inherited property, vacant homes, tired landlords, deferred maintenance, back taxes. We make a written offer after we review the property and the numbers, and a licensed Georgia attorney or title company handles closing.

Businesses

How selling a business works

Process

From first note to closing in five steps

The same structure on both tracks. You stay in control and can stop at any step.

01

Tell us about it

A short note with the address, or your industry and annual earnings. Confidential from the first message.

02

We review the numbers

For property: condition, comparable sales, repairs and title. For a business: financial statements, customers, operations.

03

Written offer

A clear price and terms in writing. For a business this is a letter of intent. For property it is a purchase agreement.

04

Due diligence

We verify what we were told, with your attorney, accountant and title company welcome at every step.

05

Close and pay

Funds go through a licensed attorney or title company. You get paid at the closing table.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you charge sellers any fees?

No. We do not charge listing fees or commissions to sellers. Normal closing costs are set out in the written agreement before you sign.

Do I have to make repairs or clean out the property?

No. We buy as-is. Tell us the condition honestly and we price accordingly.

Are you a real estate broker?

No. We buy as a principal for our own account and we say so in every agreement. You are free to talk to a broker or attorney first.

How is a business valued?

Mostly by earnings. See the indicative ranges and the estimator on the Sell a business page.
