Guides

Short guides

What is an as-is sale?

The buyer takes the property in its current condition. You skip repairs, cleaning and showings. The trade-off is a lower price than a fully renovated retail sale. Always compare both routes and take advice.

Selling an inherited house

Probate rules differ by state. Usually you need authority from the estate before selling. Talk to the estate attorney first; we are happy to work with them.

Preparing a business for sale

Clean, consistent books are the biggest driver of price and speed. Three years of financials, a customer list and clear owner roles help most.

How tax sales work

In Georgia, counties auction properties for unpaid taxes. Owners generally have a redemption period, and buyers need a lawyer to clear title. They are not shortcuts to cheap property.

Assignment of contract

An investor under contract may assign its rights to another buyer. In Georgia, sellers should be told in writing. We disclose it in our agreements.

What a closing attorney does

In Georgia, an attorney or title company handles title work, payoffs, funds and recording at closing.
