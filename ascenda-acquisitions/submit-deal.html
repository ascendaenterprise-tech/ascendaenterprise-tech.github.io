Common questions

Before you submit

Do you pay a fee for deals?

If we buy or take an assignment from you, any fee is agreed in writing before closing. We do not charge fees to submit.

Do I need to be under contract?

No, but an assignable contract helps. We can also look at properties you have found.

Are you a broker?

No. We buy and sell for our own account. We are not a licensed real estate brokerage and do not represent sellers or buyers.
