About

Building research, infrastructure and long-horizon opportunities.

Izaiah Cottle is the Founder, Owner and Chief Executive Officer of Ascenda Enterprise Capital LLC (AEC), an investment and research company focused on quantitative markets, private equity, real estate and long-duration research opportunities.

His work centers on building AEC's investment and research infrastructure, developing systematic market frameworks, evaluating private-market and real-asset opportunities, and establishing relationships with investors, operators and institutional counterparties.

Within AEC, Cottle leads development of AECQS — Ascenda Enterprise Capital's proprietary quantitative research, analysis, portfolio-risk and execution architecture. The platform is designed to evaluate multiple strategy families through a unified research framework rather than depend on a single signal or market regime.

Across AEC's areas of work, the operating principle is consistent: research first, understand the risk, build durable infrastructure and evaluate capital allocation with a long-term perspective.
