Research governance

Controls before conclusions.

AECQS is organized so research discovery, portfolio construction, implementation assumptions and review can be challenged independently.

Version control

Named configurations, dated checkpoints and separated research mandates reduce ambiguity between iterations.

Model-risk disclosure

Cost sensitivity, survivorship bias, capacity constraints and implementation gaps are stated alongside headline figures.

Evidence separation

Historical simulation, development testing and any actual execution records are treated as different evidence classes.

Controlled distribution
