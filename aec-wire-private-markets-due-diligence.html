Private Markets Due Diligence

September 4, 2026 · By Izaiah Cottle

Private-market research should examine the business model, industry, financial characteristics, management, transaction terms, risks and strategic fit. Business quality, price and transaction structure should be evaluated together.

Research framework

AEC approaches this subject through documented assumptions, scenario analysis, risk awareness and evidence-based review. Conclusions should remain proportional to the quality and limits of the underlying evidence.
