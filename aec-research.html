Research Lifecycle

A hypothesis is not a strategy until it survives challenge.

01
Hypothesis RegistryState the proposed market behavior, expected mechanism, required data and conditions that would invalidate the idea.
02
Deterministic ImplementationTranslate the idea into explicit rules for entries, exits, filters, position logic and assumptions.
03
Historical EvaluationMeasure trade behavior, drawdown, distribution, costs and sensitivity across the available historical environment.
04
Stress & RobustnessChallenge the result through parameter neighborhoods, subperiods, adverse cost assumptions and market-state segmentation.
05
Portfolio EvaluationAssess overlap, competing signals, concentration, exposure and incremental portfolio contribution.
06
Research DecisionClassify as exploratory, accepted, restricted or retired based on the current body of evidence.
07
MonitoringTrack whether the assumptions and behavior supporting acceptance continue to hold as the system evolves.
Validation Framework

What AECQS should challenge before promotion.

Sample Separation

Use in-sample work for development and preserve distinct out-of-sample or forward-style evaluation where the research design allows it.

Parameter Sensitivity

Prefer stable neighborhoods over isolated parameter peaks. A result dependent on one narrow setting deserves greater skepticism.

Subperiod Stability

Examine whether performance is concentrated in a narrow date window or persists across materially different periods.

Regime Segmentation

Measure how behavior changes across volatility, trend, structure, time-of-day and participation conditions.

Cost & Slippage Stress

Test whether the thesis remains viable after realistic fees, slippage, sizing and execution assumptions are applied.

Failure Analysis

Study losing clusters and failure modes to distinguish a weak hypothesis from a potentially repairable implementation.

Research Controls

Guardrails against accidental curve fitting.

One change at a time

Where practical, isolate modifications so observed improvement can be attributed to a specific change.

Neighboring parameters

Look for structural stability rather than celebrating a single optimum.

Preserve failed tests

Negative results are part of the research record and reduce repeated rediscovery of rejected ideas.

Investigate before retirement

A weak strategy can be examined for identifiable failure modes; improvement still must survive validation.

Portfolio contribution

Evaluate a strategy by how it interacts with other accepted components, not just in isolation.

Research ≠ live

Backtests and simulated checkpoints remain separately classified from any actual executed record.
