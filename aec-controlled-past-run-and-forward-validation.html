Forward validation

ItemStatus
StatusIn progress on paper only. The first log entry is as of the November 13, 2026 close, for the November 16 session. Nothing dated before it counts as forward. Early results are shown below, with their limits.
MethodEach day after the close, next-session target positions are computed and appended to a hash-chained log with the SHA-256 of the data and the code and a UTC write time. Editing any past entry breaks verification.
CoverageThree rule-based sleeves are logged (livestock relative momentum, FX cross mean reversion, cross-sectional skewness). This is a partial record, not the whole book. The other sleeves are being ported.
By account sizeThe log records positions, not dollars. Each of the $1M, $25M and $100M accounts will be scored from the same logged positions at its own modeled cost. Scored results by size are shown in the row below.
CheckpointsPre-registered at 60, 120 and 250 trading days after the first entry (about mid-February 2027, mid-May 2027 and November 2027). A sleeve is flagged if it falls below its 5th-percentile bootstrap band at a checkpoint. The bands are wide.
Change logOn November 17, 2026 an audit found a look-ahead in an FX clipping step (effect about 0.005% of a position unit). It was fixed, the new code hash is recorded in the log, and the log was not restarted.
Results so farAs of the November 19, 2026 close, 36 trading days, livestock, FX and skewness sleeves only (not the whole book), paper, modeled market impact. October 1 to November 13 (out-of-sample, not hash-chained): +13.1% at $1M, +12.7% at $25M, +12.3% at $100M. November 16 to 19 (logged): -1.35%, -1.38% and -1.43%. Combined: +11.8%, +11.4% and +10.8%. Most of the gain is one lean-hog move in the livestock sleeve. Thirty-six days cannot establish an edge. The first pre-registered checkpoint is day 60.
Before the logData from October 1 to November 13, 2026 (the sleeve research used data to September 30; this later data was first seen on November 14, after the rules were fixed) is out-of-sample but is not hash-chained and is not part of the forward record. It is shown separately. The log entries for November 17 to 19 were written on November 20, after those sessions; each uses only data up to its own date, but they were not written ahead of the session.

Known limits: daily data is Databento history spliced to Yahoo daily returns after September 30, 2026, and roll-gap risk in the Yahoo livestock data is unverified. Market impact cannot be measured with paper trading. Forward results do not verify the full system.
