Hypothetical performance

Simulated results are not real results.

  • All AECQS figures are simulated backtests prepared with the benefit of hindsight. They are not actual trading, not audited, and not investor returns or assets under management.
  • No live or paper track record exists for AECQS configurations. A prospective test is planned but has no results.
  • Settings and components were chosen on the same history, and many variants were tried. This creates a risk of overfitting; results on new data can be materially worse.
  • Costs, slippage, market impact and margin are modeled, not measured from live fills. Capacity, margin and intraday liquidation feasibility are not verified.
  • The drawdown limit is an objective measured on daily closes in a simulation. It is not a guarantee, and actual drawdowns could be larger. In a first-half/second-half check, second-half drawdowns exceeded the 16% limit.
  • Equity data may carry survivorship bias. Figures can change as research continues, and corrections are made when errors are found; a prior volatility overlay was removed after a data-source error.
  • Actual results would differ and could involve total loss.
