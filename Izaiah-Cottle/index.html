Leadership

Izaiah Cottle

Founder, Owner and Chief Executive Officer of Ascenda Enterprise Capital LLC. Cottle directs quantitative research development, company strategy, opportunity evaluation and institutional relationship building.

Mandate

Founder-led research and infrastructure.

Within AEC, Cottle leads the development of AECQS, the company's proprietary quantitative research, portfolio-risk, modeled-execution and validation architecture. The platform is designed to evaluate multiple systematic strategy families through a governed framework rather than depend on one signal or market regime.

AEC's operating principle is to define the hypothesis, understand the risk, document the evidence and build durable infrastructure before pursuing scale.

Quantitative research

Systematic research design, multi-asset portfolio construction and model governance.

Company strategy

Long-horizon infrastructure, ownership decisions and institutional positioning.

Counterparty relations

Engagement with institutions, family offices, strategic acquirers and research partners.

Public-record boundary

Research is not a track record.
