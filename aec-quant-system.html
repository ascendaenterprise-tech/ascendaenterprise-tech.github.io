Architecture

Seven governed research layers.

01

Research

Hypotheses, features, data requirements and experiment records.

02

Strategy

Rules, filters, entries, exits and controlled variants.

03

Regime

Trend, volatility, structure, time and participation states.

04

Signal

Rule-based confluence and candidate confidence states.

05

Risk

Exposure, sizing, concentration and drawdown constraints.

06

Execution

Deterministic implementation with explicit cost assumptions.

07

Validation

Robustness, diagnostics, stress testing and monitoring.

Governance sequence

Discovery is not acceptance.

01DefineDocument the hypothesis, eligible markets, data and failure conditions.
02ImplementEncode deterministic rules and preserve the research version.
03ChallengeTest subperiods, parameters, costs, slippage and market states.
04IntegrateEvaluate interaction, concentration and shared-account risk.
05MonitorRetain diagnostics; restrict, revise or retire when evidence changes.
