System Architecture

A multi-stage quantitative research and execution architecture.

AECQS is designed as an integrated architecture rather than a single trading strategy. Each layer has a distinct job: generating hypotheses, measuring regimes, combining signals, controlling portfolio risk, executing rules and validating whether research survives realistic constraints.

01

Research Engine

Generates, transforms and evaluates quantitative hypotheses across historical market data.

02

Strategy Engine

Encodes strategy families, parameter sets, entries, exits, filters and position logic.

03

Regime Analysis

Classifies market context using volatility, trend, structure, time and participation conditions.

04

Signal & Confluence

Combines independent evidence including structure, liquidity, order flow, momentum and statistical conditions.

05

Portfolio & Risk

Controls exposure, sizing, strategy interaction, drawdown behavior and portfolio-level constraints.

06

Execution Engine

Translates approved signals into deterministic execution logic with defined operational controls.

07

Validation & Monitoring

Tracks robustness, failure modes, out-of-sample behavior and ongoing system diagnostics.

Research Program

Broader than one model, one indicator or one market condition.

Market Structure

Structure & Liquidity

Research includes higher-timeframe location, prior highs and lows, liquidity interaction, displacement, breaks of structure, retests and response behavior around key market locations.

Statistical Research

Mean Reversion & Distribution

Statistical components evaluate standardized deviation, reversion behavior, volatility regimes, distribution shifts and the conditions under which statistical relationships remain useful.

Participation

Order Flow & Volume

AECQS research incorporates participation data and concepts such as aggressive buying/selling, absorption, exhaustion, volume concentration and liquidity response where suitable data is available.

Trend

Momentum & Continuation

Momentum and continuation families evaluate directional persistence, breakout quality, volatility expansion, multi-timeframe alignment and trend-state filters.

Value

VWAP & Market Location

Value-oriented research uses VWAP, deviation bands, profile concepts and contextual price location to distinguish extension, acceptance, rejection and reversion environments.

Portfolio Engineering

Strategy Interaction

Individual models are evaluated not only in isolation but also by how they interact, compete for entries, concentrate risk and change overall portfolio behavior.

Research Workflow

From idea to accepted system behavior.

01

Hypothesis

Define a market behavior or structural relationship that can be stated clearly enough to test.

02

Implementation

Translate the hypothesis into deterministic rules, data requirements and measurable outcomes.

03

Historical Testing

Measure trade behavior, profitability, drawdown, distribution, regime dependence and failure modes across historical data.

04

Stress & Robustness

Challenge parameters, assumptions and execution logic to reduce dependence on fragile or overly specific conditions.

05

Portfolio Integration

Evaluate whether a strategy improves the broader portfolio after exposure, overlap and shared-entry constraints are considered.

06

Monitoring

Track the behavior of accepted research as the system evolves, with the expectation that models can be revised, suspended or replaced as evidence changes.

Development Philosophy
“The objective is not to make a backtest look perfect. The objective is to build a research process strong enough to reveal what survives.”

Evidence before promotion.

AECQS development emphasizes empirical iteration, one-change-at-a-time testing where practical, explicit risk accounting, separation of research from live performance, and continuous examination of why strategies work or fail. Weak strategies are not automatically discarded solely because they lose; the research process first attempts to identify whether the underlying behavior can be improved without curve fitting.

Research vs. LiveBacktest and simulation data are labeled separately from any documented live trading activity.
