Ascenda Enterprise Capital LLC (AEC) is a private, multi-strategy investment firm that works across global economic markets. Its activities span real estate, private equity, high-capital day trading, and quantitative trading research and development.
Who we are

One owner-operated firm, four disciplines.

AEC is owned and operated by Izaiah Cottle, founder and principal. The firm is a Georgia limited liability company. It invests its own capital and develops its own research; it is not a bank, a registered investment adviser or a broker-dealer, and this website is not an offer of securities (see Disclosures).

How we work

Evidence before claims.

  • Every performance figure is labelled hypothetical when it is simulated, and is never presented as a live record.
  • Research assumptions, costs and limitations are documented and disclosed alongside results.
  • Material is shared with qualified counterparties under a non-disclosure agreement, with reproduction instructions for independent checking.
  • Figures are revised when errors are found, and corrections are stated openly.
