AEC Quant

Quantitative Research & Systematic Markets

AEC Quant is the quantitative research division of Ascenda Enterprise Capital. Its work focuses on systematic market research, strategy development, testing infrastructure, and the study of futures markets.

AEC Quant System (AECQS)

AECQS is AEC's proprietary systematic futures research platform. Published AECQS figures are historical backtest/simulated research results and do not represent audited or live investment performance.

Research principles

AEC emphasizes reproducibility, risk analysis, transparent separation of simulated research from executed results, and continued testing rather than performance guarantees.
