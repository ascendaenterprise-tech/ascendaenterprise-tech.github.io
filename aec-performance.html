Research Configurations

Two frozen configurations. One research core.

AECQS research is consolidated into two frozen configurations that share the same futures research core, equity momentum sleeve and validation standard, and differ in risk budget, capital allocation and execution capacity.
Configuration I

AECQS Controlled

The capital-discipline configuration. Separate futures and equity capital buckets, a cost-survival strategy filter, capital-scaled futures sizing under per-order and margin limits, and a two-stage drawdown throttle governed by a hard closed-day drawdown objective.

Institutional diligence candidate Prospective validation pending

Configuration II

AECQS Growth

The growth configuration. The same research core with a wider risk budget, a larger share of futures profits directed to the equity momentum sleeve, and extended execution-capacity levels that step up only when live fills confirm modeled slippage.

Validation Standard

How each configuration was tested.

01
Pre-registrationImprovements, cost model and success criteria written and hash-sealed before any result was computed.
02
Walk-forward selectionAnchored walk-forward: each year's settings chosen only from data available before that year.
03
Realistic costs & capacitySlippage, square-root market impact, margin ceilings, per-order futures limits and equity participation caps applied throughout.
04
Overfitting controlsCombinatorially symmetric cross-validation (PBO), deflated Sharpe ratio with every trial counted, and superior-predictive-ability testing.
05
Stress batteryThirty-plus scenarios: cost and impact escalation, edge haircuts, missed trades, capacity cuts, market removal, survivorship drag and combined adverse cases.
06
Sealed evidenceFrozen policies, trade ledgers and engines are fingerprinted so any forward record can be verified against the tested system.

Evidence Classification

Historical Simulation

Backtest outputs generated from historical data under modeled strategy and execution assumptions.

Development Checkpoint

A snapshot of the system at a particular research state; later versions may materially change.

Executed Record

Actual executed trading should be reported separately and only when source records support the claim.

Reporting Policy

Calculated, documented, distributed privately.

Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, drawdown profiles, rolling return distributions, win rates, trade-level attribution, benchmark statistics, stress results and overfitting diagnostics have been calculated from daily equity curves and trade ledgers using a documented methodology. Because every figure is historical backtest/simulated research, Ascenda distributes them only in institutional research reports to qualified counterparties under NDA, each carrying its research classification and hypothetical-performance disclosures.

They show

How each frozen configuration behaved historically under its modeled costs, capacity limits and walk-forward selection.

They can support

Diligence, comparison between configurations, and the design of prospective shadow and paper validation.

They do not prove

Future profitability, live execution quality at scale, investor returns or persistence in unseen markets.
