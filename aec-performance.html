Evidence Classification

Keep unlike evidence separate.

Historical Simulation

Backtest outputs generated from historical data under modeled strategy and execution assumptions.

Development Checkpoint

A snapshot of the system at a particular research state; later versions may materially change.

Executed Record

Actual executed trading should be reported separately and only when source records support the claim.

Interpretation

What these numbers do — and do not — establish.

They show

The output of the current historical research implementation under its modeled assumptions.

They can support

Comparison between research checkpoints and prioritization of further validation work.

They do not prove

Future profitability, live execution quality, investor returns or persistence in unseen markets.

Metrics Not Yet Published

No invented statistics.

Sharpe, Sortino, expectancy, rolling return distributions, strategy attribution, regime attribution and other advanced statistics should be added only after they are calculated from the underlying accepted-trade and equity-curve data using a documented methodology. This page intentionally does not estimate them from incomplete summary figures.
