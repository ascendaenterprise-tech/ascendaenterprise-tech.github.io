AEC Private Equity

We buy and build private businesses

Ascenda Enterprise Capital acquires profitable, owner-operated businesses in Georgia and New York. We evaluate each opportunity through disciplined research and due diligence, and we hold and grow what we buy.

Investment research

The process emphasizes business quality, market structure, financial characteristics, strategic fit, downside considerations, and long-term value creation.

Strategic relationships

AEC seeks thoughtful relationships with founders, operators, investors, and institutions where research, capital strategy, or transaction expertise can create mutual value.

Acquisitions

What we look for

  • Profitable businesses with a history of steady earnings, generally at least $500K a year.
  • Owners planning to retire or step back, with a transition period we can work through together.
  • Repeat or recurring customers, with no single customer making up a large share of revenue.
  • Simple operations in services, distribution, light manufacturing, healthcare support, property services and logistics.
  • Primary markets: metro Atlanta and the Hudson Valley, including Rockland and Orange County.

How we work with sellers

  • A direct conversation with the buyer, with confidentiality under an NDA before you share financials.
  • A written letter of intent with price, structure and timing, then confirmatory due diligence.
  • Flexible structures, including seller notes and a paid transition period for the current owner.
  • Your employees, customers and name are treated with care.
